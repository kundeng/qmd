# Writer lock: generalize the embed lock; fail fast first, delegate later

Status: decided by the agent; the owner may reverse.

## Decision

`src/cli/embed-lock.ts` becomes `src/daemon/writer-lock.ts`. The lock file is `.qmd-writer.lock` beside the index (the same place `.qmd-embed.lock` lives today) and holds a JSON record `{pid, mode, startedAt}`. `qmd update`, `qmd embed`, `qmd collection add/remove/rename` and `qmd cleanup` take it for their run; the daemon takes it for its life. In sprint 01 a CLI writer that finds the lock held by a live process prints who holds it and exits 1. In sprint 03 `qmd update` and `qmd embed` connect to a daemon holder and run through it instead.

## What was checked

- `embed-lock.ts` already has pid-identity recovery (`isQmdMcpPid`); the writer lock keeps it.
- `vectorIndex` (`src/cli/qmd.ts:2399`) is the only caller; `EMBED_LOCK_BUSY_MESSAGE` is asserted in `test/embed-lock.test.ts`.
- `updateCollections`, `indexFiles` and `runCleanup` have no lock today.
- Reads (`search`, `query`, `get`) and the `llm_cache` writes they make are left unlocked; `busy_timeout` 120 s covers those small writes.

## Options

| Option | Consequence | Reversal cost |
|---|---|---|
| Beside-the-index lock file, generalized from the embed lock (chosen) | one lock for every index writer; smallest diff; same location users already know | low |
| Lock in the runtime dir with the daemon files | two directories to look in for one index | low |
| SQLite-level advisory lock (`BEGIN IMMEDIATE` held) | blocks readers' checkpoints; no clear message to a second writer | medium |
